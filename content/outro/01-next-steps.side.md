---
title: More projects to try
---

If blinking an LED whetted your appetite, here are three small projects that
build on the same skeleton:

- **Reaction timer** — light an LED, then time how long until a button is pressed.
  Reuses `digitalRead()`, `millis()`, and a single resistor.
- **Knight Rider** — line up five LEDs and sweep a "scanner" light across them
  with a `for` loop and a small array of pin numbers.
- **Morse code beacon** — turn the blink into dots and dashes and send your
  initials. Just `delay()` and a lookup table.

Each one fits in a single sketch and uses only parts you already have.
