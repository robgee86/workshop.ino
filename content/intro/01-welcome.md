# Welcome 👋

By the end of this short workshop you'll have an LED blinking on an Arduino board —
and you'll understand every line that makes it happen.

## How this handbook works

- Move through the workshop with the **‹ Prev / Next ›** buttons at the bottom of each page.
- Your progress bar (top right) fills in as you complete **milestone checkpoints**.
- Some steps offer a **◇ side quest** — an optional detour. You can always come back.
- Everything works **offline** over the local network. Bookmark the link your instructor shared.

> Tip: code blocks have a **Copy** button — hover over them.

Ready? Hit **Next ›**.
